# Changelog

## 0.1.0

First release.

- OpenType `MATH` font reader with the bundled STIX Two Math font.
- LaTeX math parser:
  - macros, environments and amsmath/amssymb symbols;
  - text mode, colors, and a subset of mhchem.
- Appendix G layout:
  - spacing, scripts, limits, fractions, radicals;
  - stretchy delimiters with glyph assemblies, accents, braces, extensible arrows, arrays.
- Platform-neutral drawing items and an SVG renderer.
- More than 750 snapshot formulas, rule tests and fuzz tests.
